Data Deletion Request - Whizion

Need to delete your data from Whizion? We respect your privacy and provide an easy way to request deletion of your personal information.

How to Request Data Deletion

To request deletion of your data from the Whizion app, please follow these steps:

  1. Send an email to our support team using the link below
  2. Include your registered email address in the request
  3. Specify what data you want deleted (or request complete deletion)
  4. We will process your request within 30 days and confirm completion
📧 Request Data Deletion

What Data Will Be Deleted

When you request data deletion, the following information will be removed:

Data Type Deletion Action Timeline
Personal Information
(Email, User ID)		 Permanently deleted Within 30 days
Uploaded Videos Permanently deleted Within 30 days
Analysis Results Permanently deleted Within 30 days
Device/Usage Data Permanently deleted Within 30 days
Crash Logs Anonymized or deleted Within 30 days

Data Retention After Deletion

After processing your deletion request:

  • ✅ All personal data will be permanently removed from our systems
  • ✅ No additional retention period - data is deleted immediately upon processing
  • ✅ Third-party services (Firebase, OpenAI, Anthropic) will also be notified to delete associated data
  • ⚠️ Legal compliance data may be retained only if required by law
Important: Data deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. If you wish to use Whizion again, you will need to create a new account.

Alternative: Account Deletion

You can also delete your entire account through the app settings, which will automatically remove all associated data.
